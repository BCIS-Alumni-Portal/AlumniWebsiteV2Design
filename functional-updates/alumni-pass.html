Alumni Pass Application Guidelines Unread

1. Purpose

The BCIS Alumni Pass grants returning graduates access to campus facilities including the alumni lounge, library, fitness center, and cafeteria during designated hours. This pass is intended for alumni who visit campus on a regular basis.

2. Eligibility

To be eligible for an Alumni Pass, applicants must:

  • Be a graduate of Beijing City International School
  • Be 18 years of age or older at the time of application
  • Have completed the Alumni Registration Form

3. Terms of Use

Pass holders agree to the following conditions:

  • The pass is non-transferable and for personal use only
  • Campus access is limited to designated alumni hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM; Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM)
  • Pass holders must present their Alumni Pass at the front gate upon each visit
  • BCIS reserves the right to revoke pass privileges at any time

4. Renewal

The Alumni Pass is valid for one calendar year from the date of issue and must be renewed annually through the Alumni Portal.

5. Lost or Damaged Pass

Report lost or damaged passes to the Alumni Relations Office. A replacement fee may apply. The original pass will be deactivated immediately upon report.

BCIS Code of Conduct Unread

Community Standards

The BCIS community is built on mutual respect, integrity, and responsibility. All members of the community, including alumni, are expected to uphold these values when on campus or representing the school.

Child Protection

BCIS operates under a comprehensive child protection framework. All adults on campus, including alumni pass holders, must:

  • Avoid unsupervised contact with current students
  • Report any concerns about student welfare to school administration
  • Follow all visitor protocols in areas where students are present
  • Maintain appropriate boundaries at all times

Campus Behavior

While on campus, alumni are expected to:

  • Respect school property and shared spaces
  • Follow staff instructions regarding facility usage
  • Maintain a professional and respectful demeanor
  • Not bring unauthorized guests without prior approval

Violations

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in temporary or permanent revocation of alumni campus access privileges. Serious violations will be reported to the appropriate authorities.
